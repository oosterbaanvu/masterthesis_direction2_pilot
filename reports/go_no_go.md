# Verdict: CONDITIONAL GO for bounded further feasibility work

This recommendation concerns continued investigation of **Pilot 2**, not an unconditional commitment to a nine-month thesis. Novelty and the central C/D hypothesis remain unestablished. The initial broad NO-GO and implied preference for Pilot 1 were too strong; see `decision_revision.md` and the preserved initial documents in `decision_history/2026-10-21-initial`. No scientific result or protocol has been changed.

## Why Pilot 1 is not the default fallback

Pilot 1's own verified reports state that natural substantive-error transfer remains unestablished. Its controlled injection results do not show a stable universal gain over the stronger baseline. Public references include imputed truth, representation artefacts, missingness ceilings and unscorable repairs; there is no qualified independently verified natural financial cell-error gold. These are methodological questions, not a shortage of software or runs. A larger executed study does not resolve them. There is currently no justified scientific preference for Pilot 1.

## What Pilot 2 has and has not established

1. **Engineering feasibility is demonstrated.** Eleven acquired sources can be scanned, safe declarative rules execute, the attributed approval workflow works, and deterministic results reproduce. This supports building and testing a bounded rule-qualification experiment.
2. **Scientific usefulness is unknown.** All 360 deterministic cases ran, but no live generated-rule C/D comparison was authorized or executed. Lack of model configuration and budget is an execution prerequisite, not evidence that the method fails. Human fixtures do not establish real model quality.
3. **Broad component novelty is unsupported.** Akella, LLM-DQR and Auto-Test contain generation, filtering, selection, generalization or augmentation ingredients. The recent Bayesian-verification abstract is particularly close. The pilot cannot claim a new algorithm from assembling those components. The bounded review and inaccessible full text also do not prove that every narrower empirical question is already answered.
4. **Ground truth needs a task-specific solution.** SEC labels include constraint participants, ambiguous coordinates and inconsistent examples. SEC does not automatically fix Pilot 1's ground-truth problem. Independently adjudicated rule semantics could provide a separate evaluation target; meaningful detection claims still require trustworthy compatible error labels.

## Three gates before a thesis commitment

**Novelty gate:** Read and compare the complete closest work and available reproduction packages. Identify a specific unresolved contrast. A candidate is retention of incremental substantive error detections from the same frozen rule candidates, conditional on existing controls under source change, including abstention and useful-rule rejection. This is a proposed research angle, not a demonstrated literature gap.

**Evaluation gate:** Establish a bounded set of independently adjudicated candidate-rule semantics and meaningful violations. Predefine how validity, applicability, incremental coverage and misleading alerts will be scored. Keep rule validity distinct from detection precision. Use SEC only for compatible logical-participation claims; resolve rights and annotation issues. Do not depend solely on synthetic cell labels or algorithm-repaired references.

**Experiment gate:** With an explicitly authorized small model budget, freeze the candidate set and permitted development context, then compare exactly the same candidates before and after qualification. Include conventional controls, a no-addition choice, separate qualification-stage ablations, accepted/rejected useful-rule counts, and source-change evaluation. Keep the 210 reserved SEC bases unopened until a separate frozen confirmatory protocol exists. No paid inference is authorized by this recommendation.

Proceed to a thesis commitment only if these gates support a defensible contribution. If they fail, stop or redesign the question; returning to Pilot 1 requires independently resolving its own validity gates.

A nine-month scope could allocate months 1–2 to these novelty/evaluation gates, 3–4 to bounded generation and baseline replication, 5–6 to qualification and source shift, 7 to confirmation, and 8–9 to analysis and writing. Gate failure should end that scope early. The broad enterprise service remains an engineering demonstrator, separate from the scientific contribution.
