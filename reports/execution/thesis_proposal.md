# Reliability of qualifying LLM-generated data-quality checks under source change

**Provisional MSc Artificial Intelligence proposal — 26 October 2026.** Decision: conditional continuation of an empirical reliability study. The executed free-model study is exploratory evidence, not a completed confirmatory thesis or a claim of algorithmic novelty.

## Problem and research question

Data-quality monitoring depends on checks whose implementation, meaning and applicability agree. A language model can produce code that executes successfully while enforcing an unjustified bound, confusing units, or implementing a different relation from its own description. Conversely, rejecting every uncertain candidate avoids promotion risk but discards potentially useful detections. Organisations introducing a new source often have schema information and incomplete documentation, but few independent labels identifying actual errors. Label-assisted selection is then an informative reference rather than a directly deployable solution.

The proposed research asks: **Under documented and undocumented changes in source contracts, units and scope, when does qualification using documentation and unlabelled data discard useful incremental detections or promote unjustified LLM-generated checks?** Comparators are technical filtering, conservative documentary matching, a development-label-assisted coverage selector and conventional controls alone. The target is an empirical failure boundary and a defensible evaluation protocol. It is not an assertion that a generic documentation filter is novel or that financial context creates a new research problem.

The work has a substantive AI component: actual language-model synthesis of executable constraints and empirical study of the reliability of selecting those proposals. Statistical profiling, safe execution and a monitoring service connect that component to practical use. Their implementation is necessary infrastructure, but scientific contribution depends on the question, independent evaluation and relation to prior work.

## Related work and provisional contribution

LeDQeR generates Great Expectations checks from a known dirty tuple, evaluates correctness and same-type coverage, and selects a small set using labelled pair coverage with a precision requirement. Removing the error-type string in JustDirty does not remove the information that the tuple is erroneous. Its inspected redundancy evaluation uses labelled data for selection; that information setting differs from qualifying proposals for an arbitrary unlabelled source. The current project has executed its unchanged GX evaluation and greedy selection components on genuine newly generated candidates. It has not reproduced the paper's full models, known-error generation protocol or headline results.

Akella et al. already combine statistical inliers, retrieved knowledge, semantic enrichment, conflict resolution, a multi-category rubric and execution repair. Auto-Test studies reusable semantic-domain constraints, false-positive-constrained coverage, unseen tables and augmentation of existing checks. SPADE selects assertions using labelled good/bad LLM-pipeline outputs. LLM-DQR provides further precedent for SQL generation, deduplication and debugging. Declarative verification and evidence-based inference also predate LLMs through systems including Deequ and HoloClean.

An exceptionally close Bayesian-inspired verification article explicitly combines semantic priors and smoothed SQL violation evidence, including shared-candidate and prior-only/SQL-only comparisons. Its full text remains inaccessible in this environment despite a renewed attempt; the abstract cannot establish precise algorithm or evaluation fidelity. Obtaining and comparing the full method is an early novelty gate. No proposed baseline will be called a faithful reproduction merely because it resembles that abstract.

A possible contribution is a controlled, independently adjudicated study of applicability under source changes, with identical candidate pools, fixed conventional controls, explicitly unequal label access, abstention and lost incremental detections reported together. It must demonstrate a previously unreported limitation of practical qualification. If the closest work already answers that question, this framing should stop or be substantially narrowed. The current implementation is a transparent baseline artifact, not evidence of priority.

## Executed feasibility evidence

The project now runs Qwen2.5-Coder-7B-Instruct, official Q4_K_M GGUF weights, through a pinned CPU llama.cpp runtime without paid inference. The weights, runtime source, prompts, generation settings, returned text, token counts and response hashes are recorded. Ollama was installed from a verified release but could not serve because its required home-directory key path is read-only. The equivalent local runtime genuinely works; Gemini credentials are unnecessary for this execution. No GPU, billed API or cloud inference fallback was used.

The original prompt smoke exposed obsolete or incomplete GX expressions. Those actual outputs were retained. A separate syntax-guided condition adds generic GX constructor documentation to the frozen source prompts; it does not silently rewrite responses or substitute manual rules. Its numeric syntax examples may anchor output bounds, so executability improvement cannot be attributed solely to a neutral syntax intervention. Subsequent held-out results do not drive prompt, model or threshold changes.

Two additional schema-only benchmark generations already yield interpretable conditional outcomes. On Hospital, technical filtering adds 18 reference-cell hits and 453 off-reference alerts above the strong conventional suite. The development-label-assisted selector retains six extra reference hits with no additional off-reference cells, sacrificing 12 potentially detected reference cells. Conservative documentary qualification adds no rules and loses the available 18 extra hits. Hospital's held-out partition contains seven provider groups, insufficient for a meaningful reported bootstrap interval here.

The published-source “strong” name is relative: Hospital still has only generic missing-token screening, while Beers additionally screens ABV/IBU lexical numeric representation. Hospital B selects a familiar ZIP/ZIP+4 format check, which a conventional baseline could implement without an LLM. Its gain is therefore above this fixed basic screen, not established above a mature source-specific business suite. That baseline gap must be resolved independently before a confirmatory AI-value claim.

On Beers, strong controls identify 152 of 691 reference cells with five off-reference cells. Technical acceptance adds 770 off-reference cells and zero reference hits; B and C match the conventional suite. This is a real negative result for this model/candidate/source configuration, not proof that all extra signals are semantically false or that generated checks never help. These cell metrics remain separate from controlled-injection row metrics and from historical SEC participant scores. Independent semantic judgments are still absent.

## Hypotheses and estimands

H1 concerns technical filtering: execution success alone will leave unjustified or inapplicable proposals, assessed by independent rule review rather than execution errors or reference disagreement alone. H2 concerns conservatism: documentary qualification will reduce additional alert burden but can discard valid checks with incremental adjudicated coverage. H3 concerns conventional controls: estimated gains will shrink when comparisons include a credible strong suite rather than missingness-only checks. H4 concerns applicability change: undocumented changes of units or scope can undermine apparently stable unlabelled evidence; explicit documentation should reduce that failure, subject to the same review standard.

These are provisional hypotheses for a new frozen study, not preregistered claims retrospectively tested by this execution. Primary estimands are the proportion of independently invalid/inapplicable rules promoted, valid/applicable rules discarded, incremental adjudicated errors beyond fixed controls, additional review burden and abstention/cannot-judge rates. Report precision and recall only with their specific target population and localization unit. Do not optimize a single metric that rewards rejecting everything. A valid rule with no observed incremental errors is distinguished from an invalid rule and from a useful rule with measured increment.

The current conservative C has an additional structural limitation: its documentary knowledge is contained in strong D, so certification plus redundancy rejection forces zero acceptance above that suite. A weak-suite gain can merely restore a known conventional contract. This is a baseline sanity check, not an empirical discovery that semantic evidence cannot help. A confirmatory study must justify what additional evidence a nontrivial qualifier can use without giving it hidden error labels or manufacturing novelty.

## Experimental design and information access

Freeze source versions, conventional controls, model revisions, prompts, candidates and splits before final selection. Candidate generation and selection should use development sources only for design choices. Reserve genuinely new source groups for confirmation; this execution's public exploratory splits cannot be relabelled as pristine confirmatory data. Group periods, variants and records sharing an entity before partitioning. Determine attainable precision at the source/entity unit before promising statistical significance.

Arm A retains supported executable candidates. Arm B uses the original LeDQeR greedy selector with development labels only and an explicit precision constraint. Arm C uses inherited documentary constraints, unlabelled alert prevalence and supported representation-stability checks, with abstention for unknown meaning. Arm D uses the fixed conventional suite alone. Every arm receives the same frozen candidate pool; B's privileged label access is reported, not concealed as an equal-information competition. Single-column cell scoring retains original pair localization; relational candidates receive a separate participating-row evaluation rather than fabricated corrupted-cell blame.

Use both a weak schema/missingness suite and a strong suite based on pre-existing source contracts and development-only historical profiles. Controls must be fixed independently of model candidates and test errors. Report raw batch workload and new alerts relative to unmodified sources separately. Where an existing source already triggers a rule, aggregate before/after subtraction can hide an additional finding; use clearly defined per-rule delta diagnostics without replacing the raw monitoring burden.

Prespecified sensitivity analyses include documentation withholding, direct/cautious instructions, repeated seeds, qualification thresholds and conventional-suite strength. Source changes must preserve explicitly stated semantics when used as invariance checks. Padding whitespace is only a diagnostic of a normalization policy, not a universal meaning-preserving transformation for every field. Documentation withholding in the current inputs leaves installed control information visible and therefore is not a zero-knowledge condition. Model output diversity at temperature zero is descriptive, not independent statistical replication.

## Data, labels and independent review

The coherent primary portfolio comprises UCI Credit, Bank Marketing and White Wine plus Hospital and Beers. Classification/default/quality targets are excluded from DQ scoring. UCI experiments distinguish operational type/domain violations, controlled deletions and scale/swap alteration recovery. The last category need not be a genuine error. Published dirty/clean discrepancies support conditional reference agreement with known completeness and representation limits. Do not pool these targets into a universal DQ F1 score.

Financial relational cases are bounded by explicit units, signs, period scope and rounding. The self-authored journal scenario demonstrates same-currency integer-cent debit/credit equality but supplies no natural financial population. Prior SEC labels identify logical participants, and FinRule tasks depend on provided principles and audited release qualifications. Reserved SEC bases remain untouched without a separate frozen confirmation protocol. Real-bank data require a distinct authorization and rights assessment; no such access is assumed.

Two competent reviewers should independently assess frozen candidates, their implementations and authoritative source context while blinded to model and selector decisions. Record validity, applicability, cannot-judge, citations and rationale. Include accepted, rejected and abstained rules. Independently adjudicate target violations for the semantic-error task instead of labelling them erroneous because a candidate flags them. Preserve initial disagreements, then adjudicate; compute agreement only after real reviews exist. With one reviewer, do not claim inter-rater reliability.

## Analysis, reproducibility and engineering

Report per-source results, paired changes relative to identical controls, burden/coverage tradeoffs and cannot-judge denominators. Conditional entity-group bootstrap intervals can describe fixed-model benchmark variability where enough groups exist; they do not establish generalization over sources, models or rule meanings. Hospital's seven groups warrant withholding such an interval. Repeated seeds, injected cells and multiple rules are dependent observations. Avoid treating them as a large independent sample or inferring universal effects from five sources.

The artifact extends the existing provider/cache/GX and monitoring-service implementations. Generated code passes a literal constructor/argument allowlist before a resource-limited worker; it is not unrestricted Python or SQL execution. Full model text, failed outputs and duplicate proposals remain inspectable. Candidates enter a proposal/validation/review lifecycle; technical checks do not authorize production activation. Local actor labels demonstrate attribution, not enterprise identity or tamper-proof access control.

Exact source/weight hashes and cached outputs support deterministic rescoring without new inference. Re-running a quantized model on different hardware may differ despite a fixed seed, and a compiled binary can vary with toolchain and CPU flags. Record those differences rather than promising bitwise generation reproducibility. Raw datasets, large weights, credentials and private records remain outside Git. No paid access is required for reproducing the current cached evaluation.

## Schedule, risks and decision gates

Month one resolves closest-work novelty, source rights, reviewers and the research question. Months two and three freeze the independent design and review pilot. Months four and five execute the accepted comparisons and blinded adjudication. Month six evaluates prespecified applicability changes; month seven evaluates reserved source groups and uncertainty. Months eight and nine integrate the artifact, complete reproduction and write the thesis.

The main risks are absent semantic labels, inadequate source diversity, overlapping prior contributions and an overly weak candidate model. A stronger free model is justified only by a defined comparison and actual resources, with a separate condition. Thirty-billion-parameter model memory fit alone is insufficient evidence of practical CPU throughput. Do not turn model expansion into a substitute for better labels or an unanswered research question.

Supervisor approval is required for the precise contribution, closest-paper adequacy, independent-review/ethics plan, rights-cleared sources, primary estimands, minimum meaningful effect and attainable source-level power. Stop or reframe if novelty or independent review fails early. A well-supported negative reliability boundary can be a defensible MSc contribution. More infrastructure, unreviewed generated rules or a favorable synthetic score cannot rescue an invalid evaluation.
