# Closest-work review — evidence available on 21 October 2026

Generating, executing, repairing, deduplicating or filtering LLM rules is not a defensible new contribution here. The candidate research question remains conditional: several papers already address important parts, and one exceptionally close article is not available in full text. This is a bounded primary-source feasibility review, not an exhaustive systematic review.

## LeDQeR details that materially change the comparison

The generator explicitly selects `isDirty == True`. Removing the explicit error-type string does not remove the knowledge that a particular tuple is erroneous. The correctness stage uses known erroneous cells/values. Coverage tests other examples of the same labelled error type; the paper's experiments apply **no minimum recall threshold**, so that stage does not discard rules there. The greedy stage requires estimated precision and labelled positive coverage. These information requirements differ from onboarding an unlabelled source.

The paper reports mean correctness-stage F1 around 0.66 and coverage-stage recall around 0.88, aggregated over its configurations. These are author-reported results, not our reproduced scores. Its final small rule sets are neither proof of semantic correctness on new sources nor directly comparable to our handwritten component fixtures. The implementation's `eval` of generated Python also requires a separate execution threat model.

## What is established and what remains uncertain

Executable generation, iterative repair, semantic/rubric filtering, statistical selection, redundancy removal and augmentation of existing checks have precedents. The inspected sources demonstrate benchmark-specific results; they do not establish universal promotion reliability. Full-text uncertainty for the Bayesian article is a reason to withhold a novelty claim, not evidence that a gap exists.

The possible contribution is a **carefully controlled empirical reliability/limitation study**: fixed genuine model candidates, a fixed conventional control suite, source-level separation, explicitly unequal label access, independent semantic adjudication, and documented versus undocumented source changes. A new acceptance algorithm is not currently established. Even this study needs a substantive distinction from Auto-Test, Akella, SPADE and Bayesian verification confirmed with the supervisor.
