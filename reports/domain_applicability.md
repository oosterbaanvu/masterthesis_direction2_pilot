# Domain applicability

Regular customer records support explicit datatypes, code lists, keys, ranges and a few cross-field conditions. Historical envelopes add scale-screening coverage but require a reference distribution. A contract violation identifies a reason to investigate; it does not prove the contract is correct. Credit status-code ambiguity, signed bills and bank sentinels prevent treating convenient narrow domains as truth.

SEC financial statements have multilevel headers, omissions for presentation, negative accounting notation, segment eliminations, units and rounding. A generic rule such as “all displayed numbers sum to the total” is unsafe. The lexical baseline has no business contract and is deliberately a limited coverage check. The missing-cell variant is an unsupported-semantics sensitivity, not an approved financial control. Published direct LLM results and this baseline use different evaluation populations in the main reports; their aggregate scores are not a head-to-head method claim.

Published dirty/clean sources span cross-source conflicts (Flights), representation changes (Beers), synthetic substitutions (Hospital), reference artefacts (Tax), ambiguous bibliography repairs (Rayyan), and missingness (Restaurant/Cars). A high completeness score on Cars does not establish a useful logical-consistency detector. A method lacking appropriate input or trustworthy labels is excluded or workload-only, never zero-filled in a pooled leaderboard.

Operational source scans demonstrate reusable findings and governance mechanics across domains. They are not cross-domain proof of rule semantic validity. The current artifact does not implement a referential-integrity connector across separate source tables, an authenticated bank workflow, or a faithful execution of dbt/Deequ/Great Expectations. Its reconciliation/FD primitives and typed source contracts provide extension points without claiming those integrations already exist.

For a thesis, keep financial logical-table, regular controlled, published-reference and operational tracks separate. Quantify incremental substantive errors beyond source controls, use independent adjudication, and distinguish transfer of a fixed rule across sources from regenerating a new rule after seeing the target schema. Neither source regeneration nor broad domain coverage alone establishes generalization.
