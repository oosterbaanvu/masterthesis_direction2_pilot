# Verdict: NO-GO for the current broad thesis formulation

This is a decision about the **current scientific proposal**, not about whether the local software works. Do not promote the broad question or the assembled pipeline as a demonstrated new MSc AI contribution.

The evidence for that verdict is:

1. **Close prior art already contains the proposed ingredients.** Akella 2025 includes rule/code generation, conflict resolution, rubric qualification and execution repair; LLM-DQR implements SQL generation, deduplication and debugging; Auto-Test studies constraint selection, false-positive control, unseen tables and augmentation. A publisher abstract dated **20 October 2026** describes shared-candidate semantic/SQL verification immediately before production monitors. Its full text must be reviewed before claiming an acceptance-layer gap.
2. **The main comparison has no empirical LLM evidence.** C and D share a working evaluation interface, but no model, usable inference binding or spend authorization was supplied. Live B, C, D and C-versus-D are NOT_RUN. Human test fixtures do not prove generation quality, semantic validity or deployment suitability. The experiment did not quietly use Codex as the experimental model.
3. **The proposed financial benchmark changes the estimand.** SEC annotations include logically involved cells, ambiguous blank-row coordinates and inconsistent examples; release rights are unstated. It is useful for a conditional logical-consistency track, but does not supply independent natural financial cell-corruption truth or semantic rule-validity labels.
4. **Executed controls reveal bounded gaps and large confounds.** All 360 deterministic cases ran; conventional controls capture the easy populations, while plausible substitutions largely survive. Historical limits improve some scale coverage but trigger heavy benign-shift workload. These are worthwhile feasibility findings, not evidence that qualification improves generated rules.
5. **Engineering success is separate.** Eleven-source scans, safe execution, an attributed approval lifecycle, batch profiles and tests establish a working local demonstrator. They do not establish scientific novelty or authenticated enterprise governance.

Pilot 1 is presently the more empirically mature direction: its verified branch contains executed core selection comparisons and documented negative/transfer findings. It also needs sharper novelty and independent-label qualification. The two pilots solve different tasks and their scores are not ranked against each other.

## Conditions for reconsidering a narrower Pilot 2

Obtain and review the complete Akella/LLM-DQR/Bayesian acceptance literature and nearest reproduction artifacts; write a specific unresolved contrast. A plausible candidate is **retention of incremental substantive error detections from the same frozen rule candidates, conditional on existing controls under source change**, including explicit abstention and useful-rule rejection. This is a proposed follow-up, not a demonstrated novel gap.

Build an independently adjudicated development/evaluation set of candidate semantics and meaningful violations. Decide whether SEC's participant-location task is scientifically compatible; otherwise narrow the benchmark role rather than inventing clean truth. Resolve data rights. Authorize a small explicit model budget, freeze generation inputs/candidates, and compare syntax/execution-only, empirical-only, semantic-only and combined qualification under equal initial candidates. Include ordinary controls and a no-addition choice. A new frozen confirmatory protocol must precede unlocking the 210 reserved SEC bases.

Within nine months, spend the first two months on these novelty/ground-truth gates, months 3–4 on bounded generation and baselines, 5–6 on qualification and source-shift analysis, 7 on confirmation, and 8–9 on analysis, demonstrator and thesis writing. Stop or return to Pilot 1 if the novelty or adjudication gates fail. Do not spend most of the thesis implementing a general governance platform.
