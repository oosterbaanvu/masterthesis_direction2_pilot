These conditional injection-budget results are cited to show baseline dependence; they are **not compared numerically with SEC table-level F1**. If P2 is selected, scientific work should isolate a frozen-candidate qualification/retention tradeoff conditional on an existing control set and source changes, after full-text novelty review and independent semantic labels. Spend early effort adjudicating rules/errors and reproducing the nearest acceptance-layer baseline, rather than adding a user interface. The broader service remains a separate demonstrator. Current evidence does not justify replacing P1 merely because P2 uses LLMs or SEC data.
